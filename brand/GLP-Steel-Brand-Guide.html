Brand Starter

GLP Steel

Lift heavy, eat protein, keep your muscle while the weight comes off. A wordmark built on a double meaning — the weight you lift and the weight you lose — and an identity engineered to look premium, feel timeless, and read instantly at any size.

Primary Logo

The icon is a barbell resolved as a steel plate badge — but read the silhouette and it is also an I-beam, the universal mark of structural steel. Strength you build, strength that endures. The two ember plates carry the accent and hint at the metabolic “burn” side of the story.

Lockups

Stacked lockup for square placements, app store and social avatars use the icon alone, and the reversed lockup covers dark UI and photography. All four are provided as editable SVG.

Graphite and steel do the heavy lifting; ember is a spark, not a flood — reserve it for one thing per screen (the accent plates, a primary button, a key number). This restraint is what keeps the brand reading as premium rather than “gym poster.”

Typography

ArchivoDisplay / Logo / Headlines — weights 800–900
InterUI / Body / Labels — weights 400–600

Both faces are free and open-source, so the app, the web, and any print stay perfectly consistent at zero licensing cost. If you later want a more bespoke feel, Neue Haas Grotesk Display or Söhne are the licensed step-up for the display face and swap in without redrawing the logo.

Usage

Do

  • ✓ Keep clear space of at least the icon’s corner radius on all sides.
  • ✓ Minimum icon size 24 px on screen; wordmark min height 20 px.
  • ✓ Use the reversed lockup on graphite, charcoal, or dark photos.
  • ✓ Let ember appear once per view.

Don’t

  • ✕ Recolor the barbell or add gradients / shadows.
  • ✕ Stretch, rotate, or re-space the wordmark.
  • ✕ Place the ink icon on a busy or low-contrast background.
  • ✕ Swap the typeface in the wordmark.
