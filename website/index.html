Questions, answered

I'm not on a GLP-1. Is this app still for me?

Absolutely. The program engine builds effective strength training for anyone with any equipment. GLP-1 users get extra attention — muscle-preservation programming and protein-first nutrition — but the fundamentals benefit everyone.

What equipment do I need?

Whatever you have. A pair of dumbbells, resistance bands, a kettlebell, a full home gym — the app photographs your gear and designs the program around it. Nothing to buy.

Is it really $15 with no subscription?

Yes. $15 once buys the full app forever, including updates. The only extras are optional $1 scans: a new equipment scan if your gear changes, or a fresh food scan for new recipes. You'll never see a recurring charge.

What happens to my photos?

Photos are used to recognize your equipment or food, and you review and confirm everything the AI finds. Images are retained only with your explicit consent. No ads, no data sales, passwordless login — privacy is a design decision, not a settings toggle.

I'm a complete beginner. Will I know what to do?

Yes — and there's a mode built for exactly this. Couch-to-Weights starts you with a single exercise a day (8–15 minutes) and adds just one more each week over 6 weeks, so you're never overwhelmed. Every exercise has a plain-English description and a how-to video, sessions guide you set by set with rest timers and pre-filled weights, and the app tells you exactly when to add weight or reps.

Can I train with no equipment at all?

Yes. Flip on bodyweight-only and you'll get a full program with nothing to buy and no photo required. You can also choose exactly which equipment to include, and scan new gear in later to fold it into your plan automatically.

Why is protein such a big deal on GLP-1s?

GLP-1 medications suppress appetite, so many users under-eat protein — the raw material for keeping muscle. When intake drops in a deficit, muscle loss accelerates. GLP Steel makes hitting your protein target realistic with small, protein-dense meals built from food you already have. (Educational info, not medical advice — talk to your clinician about your specific needs.)

Which platforms are supported?

iOS today, with Android on the way. Your account and history sync securely, so you can switch devices without losing a single set.
